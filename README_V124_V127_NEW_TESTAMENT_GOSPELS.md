# V124–V127 — New Testament Deep Guides: The Four Gospels

This update continues from V123, which completed all 39 Protestant Old Testament deep guides.

## Added
- Matthew — 28 chapters
- Mark — 16 chapters
- Luke — 24 chapters
- John — 21 chapters
- Total: 89 new chapter-level guides

## Historical design
The Gospel guides distinguish:
- Gospel narrative and theological claims
- first-century historical context
- Roman and Herodian political structures
- Second Temple Jewish institutions and movements
- archaeology and external evidence where appropriate
- scholarly questions about authorship, dating and sources
- later Christian interpretation

No copyrighted Bible translation text is copied into the application. Chapter pages continue to link outward to the Bible reference service already used by the project.

## Special emphasis
Matthew: Jewish Scripture, kingdom teaching, Herodian/Roman Judea, Jerusalem and the Temple.
Mark: suffering Messiah, Galilean ministry, Roman execution context, textual history of Mark 16.
Luke: poverty, social status, women, Samaria, Jerusalem and the Luke–Acts connection.
John: festivals, Jerusalem chronology, distinctive signs/discourses, high Christology and careful treatment of first-century Jewish identity.
