# Biblical History — V10–V12 Combined

This release combines three major content updates into one package.

- **V10:** Divided Kingdoms & Assyria — Rehoboam through the fall of Samaria, including Omri, Ahab, Elijah, Elisha, Jehu, Mesha, Tiglath-Pileser III, Hezekiah, Sennacherib, Lachish, Isaiah, Manasseh, Josiah and Nineveh.
- **V11:** Babylon, Exile & Persian Return — Jehoiakim, Jehoiachin, Zedekiah, Jerusalem 586 BCE, Exile, Daniel, Belshazzar, Cyrus, Zerubbabel, Ezra, Nehemiah and Esther.
- **V12:** Hellenistic, Roman & New Testament World — Alexander, Ptolemies, Antiochus IV, Maccabees, Hasmoneans, Pompey, Herod, John the Baptist, Jesus, Pilate, Paul, James, the First Jewish Revolt, destruction of Jerusalem and Masada.

V9 content remains intact. The new pages use the same article schema and are intentionally structured like encyclopedia/wiki entries, with explicit separation between Biblical narrative, historical context, archaeology and uncertainty.
