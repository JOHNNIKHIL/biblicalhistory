# Biblical History V13–V15

Combined three-milestone release.

## V13 — Persian Empire & Restored Community
Darius I, Xerxes I, Artaxerxes I, Yehud, Elephantine and Second Temple completion.

## V14 — Hellenistic World
Diadochi, Ptolemaic Judea, Seleucid Judea, Antiochus IV, Dead Sea Scrolls and Hasmonean expansion.

## V15 — Roman Judea, Jesus & Revolts
Pompey, Augustus, Herod's Temple, Herod's successors, John the Baptist, Jesus in Galilee, crucifixion, resurrection tradition, Paul, James, First Jewish Revolt, Jerusalem 70 CE, Masada and Bar Kokhba.

The release is built on V10–V12 and preserves all previous story files.
