# V64–V66 — Kingdoms, Empires & Political History

Built additively on V61–V63.

## V64 — Political entities database
Added `content/kingdoms/index.ts` with structured entries for major powers and political entities across the Biblical world:
- Egypt
- Hittite Empire
- Neo-Assyrian Empire
- Kingdom of Israel
- Kingdom of Judah
- Aram-Damascus
- Moab
- Ammon
- Edom
- Philistine city-states
- Neo-Babylonian Empire
- Achaemenid Persia
- Macedonian / Hellenistic Greek world
- Ptolemaic Kingdom
- Seleucid Empire
- Hasmonean state
- Roman Empire
- Herodian kingdom

Each entity includes dates, capital, region, Biblical significance, historical significance, external evidence, rulers, associated people/places, Biblical references and themes.

## V65 — Kingdom explorer
Added `/kingdoms` with search and era filtering, entity cards and political-history framing.

## V66 — Individual political profiles
Added `/kingdoms/[slug]` with:
- political identity and dates
- capital and region
- Biblical significance
- historical significance
- external evidence / archaeology
- rulers
- connected people
- associated places
- Biblical references
- themes

Header navigation now includes Kingdoms.

The layer deliberately separates Biblical narrative from independently attested historical evidence and avoids treating every biblical political detail as externally verified.
