# V67–V69 — Archaeology & Primary Evidence

## V67 — Evidence Library
Added `content/evidence/index.ts` with a structured evidence layer covering inscriptions, royal records, chronicles, manuscripts, archaeological sites, reliefs, seals/objects and architectural evidence.

The initial library includes major records and sites such as the Merneptah Stele, Tel Dan Stele, Mesha Stele, Black Obelisk, Kurkh Monoliths, Sennacherib's Prism, Lachish Reliefs, Siloam Inscription, Ketef Hinnom silver scrolls, Babylonian Chronicles, Cyrus Cylinder, Dead Sea Scrolls, Pilate Stone, Hezekiah's Tunnel, Lachish, Hazor and more.

Each record distinguishes:
- why the evidence matters
- what it can actually establish
- limits of interpretation
- Biblical references
- connected people, places and political entities
- related encyclopedia articles

## V68 — Evidence Explorer
Added `/evidence` with search and type filtering across the evidence library.

## V69 — Individual Evidence Profiles
Added `/evidence/[slug]` with encyclopedia-style profiles and cross-links to People, Places, Kingdoms and related historical articles.

Updated the main navigation with **Evidence**.

No existing content was intentionally removed; V67–V69 was built directly on the V64–V66 project.
