# V6 Research Notes

## Scope

V6 follows the transition from the late Assyrian period into the Neo-Babylonian
Empire and the destruction/exile sequence affecting Judah.

Major anchors:

- 681 BCE — Sennacherib's assassination
- 680–669 BCE — Esarhaddon
- 669–631 BCE — Ashurbanipal
- 626 BCE — Nabopolassar
- 612 BCE — fall of Nineveh
- 609 BCE — final Assyrian collapse / Josiah's death context
- 605 BCE — Carchemish
- 597 BCE — Jerusalem captured / Jehoiachin deported
- 588 BCE — final Jerusalem siege begins
- 586 BCE — Jerusalem and First Temple destroyed
- c. 582 BCE — later deportation / Gedaliah period
- 561 BCE — Jehoiachin receives royal allowance
- 604–562 BCE — Nebuchadnezzar's Babylonian building program

## External source families

The project treats these as distinct evidence streams:

1. Babylonian Chronicles
2. Neo-Babylonian royal inscriptions and foundation cylinders
3. Assyrian royal inscriptions
4. Jerusalem / Judah archaeology
5. Babylonian administrative tablets
6. Hebrew Bible
7. Later classical and Jewish historical sources where appropriate
