# Biblical History V79–V81 — Extended Scripture Library

## V79 — Extended Scripture metadata
Added `content/extended/books.ts` with structured records for major deuterocanonical, Orthodox and Ethiopian-tradition works and textual additions.

Included: Tobit, Judith, Wisdom of Solomon, Sirach, Baruch, 1 Maccabees, 2 Maccabees, Additions to Esther, Additions to Daniel, 1 Esdras, 3 Maccabees, Psalm 151, Prayer of Manasseh, 4 Maccabees, 1 Enoch, Jubilees, and 1–3 Meqabyan.

## V80 — Extended Scripture explorer
Added `/extended` with grouped libraries, tradition badges, historical periods, summaries, and links into individual study pages.

## V81 — Individual study pages
Added `/extended/[slug]` pages with tradition status, structure, historical-literary lens, research notes, connections, and links to Canon and Connections.

## Preservation
Built additively from V76–V78. Existing 66-book Bible, canon comparison, people, places, kingdoms, evidence, events and knowledge-graph layers are preserved.

## Scope
This release adds metadata and study context. It does not reproduce full Bible/deuterocanonical translations. Structure/counts are labelled as approximate or variable where traditions and editions differ.
